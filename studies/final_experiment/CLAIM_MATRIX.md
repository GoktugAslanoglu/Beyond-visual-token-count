Historical GLM/LONG was selected because it was the existing positive operating point; this selection must be acknowledged in every report. All validly completed outcomes are reported.

None of these outcomes changes the validity of the matched-allocation layout intervention. There is no isolated causal claim about serialization versus retention: the primary contrast compares complete storage/readout policies. The original hash arm provides a descriptive fresh-sample anchor, not a second primary hypothesis. A negative or inconclusive result is valuable because it bounds the practical interpretation rather than requiring a new architecture or a new paper.
